How I work

The habits behind every project on this page.

I plan before I code

I draw the data model and list the endpoints first. RaceDay started with an ERD and an API plan before any C# was written.

I test as I go

My projects use unit tests and GitHub Actions, so a mistake shows up on the next push and not on the day I hand it in.

I keep things simple

I write code in small parts with one job each, and I explain my work in plain English. If I cannot explain it simply, I do not understand it yet.

I take feedback seriously

When my lecturer reviewed our project plan, we rebuilt it point by point. A plan only gets better when someone challenges it.

Your Vision. Our Creation.

Drew Technologies is a South African technology and digital solutions business. It helps businesses, entrepreneurs, startups and individuals build and strengthen their presence in the digital world.

We believe every business deserves modern, professional and reliable technology. We turn ideas into practical digital experiences through creative design, software development and innovative technology.

Founder

Abongile Lathitha Manqana

Drew Technologies was founded by Abongile Lathitha Manqana, with a vision of using technology, creativity and software development to help businesses grow and bring their ideas to life.

The business is driven by an interest in software development, digital innovation and the opportunities technology creates. Through Drew Technologies, the aim is to build a growing technology brand that provides practical digital solutions while continuously exploring new technologies.
